### C-01: mobile layout rationale

The off-board source contains a 678px introduction, 920px plan group and 587px empty BMI frame. The 2305px total is approximately 24% of the mobile board's height; this is source geometry, not a measured runtime page. Compact plan cards keep both offers visible; an inline BMI disclosure lets interested users expand the full form without leaving the page. Do not split the three parts into mutually exclusive tabs, hide the entire service, or force a scroll jump on expansion. A default section height of roughly 1100–1400px is an initial design budget, not a fixed height or verified result. P5 measured the 375px implementation at 1407.7px from the Weight Loss introduction through the collapsed BMI, including the intervening 56px gap. This is about 897px shorter than the source-only 2305px stack; it is not a fixed height. Both offers remain visible. The Semaglutide thumbnail is withheld under the approved asset policy; see C-12.

This applies general findings from [NN/g on mobile accordions](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-accordions/), [GOV.UK's tab guidance](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/tabs/) and [Baymard's mobile product-page research](https://baymard.com/research-articles/avoid-horizontal-tabs). They support keeping comparison content visible and making secondary content optional; they are not Apsu-specific user-test results.
