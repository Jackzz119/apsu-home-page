### C-01: mobile layout rationale

The off-board source contains a 678px introduction, 920px plan group and 587px empty BMI frame. The 2305px total is approximately 24% of the mobile board's height; this is source geometry, not a measured runtime page. Compact plan cards keep both offers visible; an inline BMI disclosure lets interested users expand the full form without leaving the page. Do not split the three parts into mutually exclusive tabs, hide the entire service, or force a scroll jump on expansion. A default section height of roughly 1100–1400px is an initial design budget, not a fixed height or verified result. P5 must measure it with real assets and text.

This applies general findings from [NN/g on mobile accordions](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-accordions/), [GOV.UK's tab guidance](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/tabs/) and [Baymard's mobile product-page research](https://baymard.com/research-articles/avoid-horizontal-tabs). They support keeping comparison content visible and making secondary content optional; they are not Apsu-specific user-test results.
